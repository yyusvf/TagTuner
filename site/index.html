    Built for real music folders.

    Edit straight from the Explorer

    Right click a few songs and choose Edit metadata. A small window opens with just those files, and closes when you are done.

    Covers from the folder

    Give a song the cover its neighbours already have. Two clicks, no image file needed.

    Six formats, one click

    Bring a whole folder to one format and sample rate.

    Every album of an artist, one view

    Open the artist folder and its albums unfold below, each with its own disc and track numbers.

    Speaks thirteen languages

    Follows your Windows language, or pick one in the settings.

    Everything in one window.

    Tags on the left, your library in the middle, the folder as a playlist, and what the folder needs on the right.

    Nothing gets lost.

    • A backup before every changeTags, covers, conversions and renames all keep the original first.
    • History with undoEvery step is listed and can be taken back, the last one with Ctrl+Z.
    • Restore single filesSee what changed in each backup and put back exactly the files you want.
